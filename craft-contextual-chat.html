Problem

Search asked for precision too early

There were 40+ filters with no hierarchy. Unless you knew the exact labels, you re-ran searches, reopened tabs and rebuilt context from memory.

Investors start broad and narrow down. The product made them translate a hunch into filter vocabulary before they'd seen anything.

Key decisions

Three calls that shaped it

01

Chat on top of search, not smarter filters

The team had two obvious answers: better filters, or a chatbot. I put chat on top of the page and kept classic search as a fallback, so nobody was forced into it on day one.

Tried
Smarter filters, a dashboard of AI picks, chat
Dropped
Smarter filters. The dashboard was parked for phase 2.
Why
Filters demand precision users didn't have yet. New investors have no signals for a dashboard, and “magic” picks weren't trusted.

02

Never open on an empty box

In test round 1, 4 of 6 users stalled for 15+ seconds at “Ask anything”. v2 added three prompt starters by role.

Tried
An empty panel with a single Let's Chat button
Dropped
The open “Ask anything” start
Why
People didn't know how to begin

03

Answers you can scan and check

Early AI accuracy sat around 74%, wrong often enough to plan for. In round 2, investors wanted to scan in under 10 seconds and skipped prose. So answers lead with a bold summary, then bullets and tables, and every claim links to the tab where its data lives.

Tried
History and table answers in a heavy overlay (v3)
Dropped
The heavy overlay and prose answers
Why
Not trusted enough for diligence

What I'd do next

Loose ends

  • Define AI confidence as a metric from day one. It only became one midway.
  • Test the first chat earlier. It was hard even for experts, and it cost a full iteration.
  • Proactive alerts on followed companies, and prompts tuned by role.
